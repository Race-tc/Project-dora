Live OBD-II Monitoring

Connect directly to the vehicle via ELM327 or simulate with built-in data. Real-time RPM, MAP, MAF, fuel trims, knock, and O2 sensor readings — all on one screen.

AI Calibration Analysis

Claude AI reads your log data and returns structured, conservative map adjustments with per-cell explanations. Targets fuel trim within ±5% and flags knock, lean conditions, and boost creep automatically.

3D Map Visualisation

Interactive 3D surface plots for Boost, Throttle, MAF, Short Term FT, and Long Term FT. Click any cell to edit. Changes are highlighted in real time before you apply them.

ROM Identification

Automatic vehicle identification from ROM binary files using a machine learning classifier trained on real ECU data. Vehicle-specific constraints applied to every recommendation.

Log Analysis Dashboard

Upload and analyse CSV data logs. Automatic channel detection, statistical summary, and per-channel plots. Identify problem RPM/load zones before touching the map.

Common questions

How do I get the software?

Join the waitlist with your email — no card needed. When the beta launches on 16 September, we email everyone a free licence key and the installer link. Paste your key into Settings and you're fully unlocked.

What hardware do I need?

A Windows PC and an ELM327 OBD-II Bluetooth or USB adapter (available for under £10 online). DORA also runs in Simulator mode with no hardware — useful for testing and training.

Is my engine data sent anywhere?

Log data is sent to our secure AI backend only when you click "Analyse". It is used solely to generate your recommendation and is not stored or shared. OBD live data never leaves your machine.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Your licence covers one workshop (one active installation). If you need to move to a new machine, just install the app and enter the same key.

What vehicles does it support?

Any vehicle with an OBD-II port (all petrol and diesel cars sold in the UK after 2001). ROM identification supports a growing database of common ECU binaries — coverage expands with each update.

What happens when the beta ends?

Beta keys work through 28 October. We'll email everyone before then with the option to move to a paid plan — nothing is charged automatically, and there's no card on file to cancel.
