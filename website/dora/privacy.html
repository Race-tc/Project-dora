This Privacy Policy explains how RACE, the workshop tools company behind DORA ("RACE", "we", "us"), collects, uses, and protects personal data when you join the DORA beta waitlist, use the DORA desktop application ("Software"), browse or upload to the Tune Marketplace, or otherwise use projectdora.com (together, the "Service").

1. Who We Are

RACE is the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. See Section 12 for how to reach us.

Live OBD-II data read from a connected vehicle stays on your machine and is never transmitted by the Software, except for the specific log data you explicitly submit for AI analysis as above. Remote Tune's live-sharing feature writes the same kind of sensor data to a local session file for a connected guest to read directly — this also never passes through RACE's own systems, though it means the file is only as private as the location it's saved to (the same machine, or a folder on a shared/synced drive); RACE has no visibility into or control over who else can access that location.

3. How We Use It

  • to create and manage your waitlist entry or licence, and issue/validate licence keys;
  • to generate the AI calibration recommendation you requested;
  • to host and display Marketplace Content you choose to upload;
  • to process payment once paid plans launch, via Stripe;
  • to respond to support requests and send service emails (e.g. your beta key, licence confirmation);
  • to keep the Service secure and diagnose faults.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not use your data for third-party advertising.

4. Legal Basis

Where UK/EU data protection law applies, we process your data under one of the following bases: contract (to provide the Service you signed up for, such as issuing a licence key or running an analysis you requested), consent (for optional communications, such as beta programme emails, which you can withdraw at any time), and legitimate interests (to keep the Service secure and functioning).

5. Sharing & Third Parties

We share personal data only with the following categories of third party, and only as needed to run the Service:

  • AI provider — engine data log content you submit is sent to a third-party large language model provider solely to generate your calibration recommendation.
  • Voice providers (OpenAI, ElevenLabs) — audio you submit via Ask Dora's voice mode is sent to these providers solely to transcribe your speech and generate a spoken reply; it's proxied through our backend and not stored beyond what's needed to process that one request.
  • Payment processor (Stripe) — handles payment once paid plans launch; we never see full card details.
  • Waitlist form provider (Google Forms) — hosts the waitlist signup form; submissions are relayed into our own systems.
  • Email delivery provider — used to send confirmation, beta key, and licence emails.
  • Hosting provider — our backend and database run on managed infrastructure (Railway).

We do not share Marketplace Content contact details (your email or licence key) publicly — public Marketplace listings never include your email address or licence key. We may disclose data where required by law.

6. Data Retention

Engine data logs submitted for AI analysis are used only to generate that recommendation and are not retained beyond what is needed to process the request. Waitlist, licence, and Marketplace data is retained for as long as your licence or Marketplace listing is active, and for a reasonable period afterwards to meet legal, accounting, and support obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

7. Your Rights

If UK/EU data protection law applies to you, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent. To exercise any of these rights, email racebuilds@gmail.com. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the ICO).

8. Cookies

projectdora.com does not use advertising or analytics tracking cookies. The waitlist signup form is embedded from Google Forms, which may set its own cookies under Google's privacy policy while that form is loaded; we do not control those.

9. International Transfers

Some of the third parties listed in Section 5 (including our AI provider and hosting provider) may process data outside the UK/EEA. Where this happens, we rely on that provider's own safeguards (such as Standard Contractual Clauses) for the transfer.

10. Children

The Service is intended for users aged 18 and over, consistent with Terms & Conditions, Section 1. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify active licence holders by email.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy, or requests relating to your personal data, can be sent to racebuilds@gmail.com.
