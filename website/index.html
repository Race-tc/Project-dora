Common questions

How do I get the software after paying?

Your licence key is emailed to you within seconds of payment. Download the installer from this page, install it, open Settings in the app, and paste your key. That's it — fully unlocked.

What hardware do I need?

A Windows PC and an ELM327 OBD-II Bluetooth or USB adapter (available for under £10 online). Project Dora also runs in Simulator mode with no hardware — useful for testing and training.

Is my engine data sent anywhere?

Log data is sent to our secure AI backend only when you click "Analyse". It is used solely to generate your recommendation and is not stored or shared. OBD live data never leaves your machine.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

Your licence covers one workshop (one active installation). If you need to move to a new machine, just install the app and enter the same key.

What vehicles does it support?

Any vehicle with an OBD-II port (all petrol and diesel cars sold in the UK after 2001). ROM identification supports a growing database of common ECU binaries — coverage expands with each update.

What if I want to cancel?

Cancel any time from the billing portal link in your welcome email — no phone calls, no forms. Your access continues until the end of the billing period.
