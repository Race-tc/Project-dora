# LinkedIn post — DORA beta launch

---

Calibrating a car today means running a logging tool, a separate map editor, a spreadsheet for dyno numbers, and a forum tab to argue about whether a spike was knock or noise — four pieces of software that don't talk to each other. The tuner is the one stitching it all together, holding the full picture in their head, rebuilding it from scratch every session, because none of the tools are willing to do it for them.

That's the gap I built DORA to close.

**DORA — AI-powered ECU calibration software for tuning garages. Beta opens September 16th.**

It's one desktop suite for the whole calibration workflow, with an AI layer that actually helps you read what's happening in the maps instead of just plotting numbers on a grid.

[screenshot: DORA splash/home screen or app icon]

**The tuning core:**
🔧 A full map editor — 3D surface view, live cell crosshair, smoothing, region fill/extrapolate/blend, multi-map compare, undo/redo, Excel import/export
📊 Real-time OBD-II monitoring with a drag-and-drop gauge grid, oscilloscope view, and GPS overlay
📈 Data logging and log analysis, including WOT analysis and knock logging with per-cell risk mapping
🏎️ A dyno simulator with SAE/DIN correction and automatic PDF reports

[screenshot: map editor with 3D surface view]

**Everything that usually needs five other apps:**
⚙️ Fuel trim, closed-loop, launch control, and flex-fuel panels
🔍 ROM tools — hex editor with multiple cipher modes, checksum correction, ROM diff, patch export, and version history you can roll back through
🧾 VIN decoding, wiring diagrams, sensor calibration, and a 56-code DTC library
🚨 A safety guard that checks every ROM write before it's allowed to happen — no bypass, by design

[screenshot: live monitor / gauge dashboard]

**Where the AI comes in:**
🤖 It flags knock risk, fuel trim drift, and tuning opportunities directly on the map, and answers plain-language questions like "what does this cell change actually do."

[screenshot: marketplace / community tune browser]

**And it doesn't stop at the desktop:**
🌐 A built-in marketplace to upload your own tunes and pull proven calibrations from the community
🌐 Role-based access so shop owners control exactly what staff can flash, edit, or view

[screenshot: dyno panel]
